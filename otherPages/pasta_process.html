Recipe : chicken and broccoli penne pasta

Step by Step full process

• Cook Pasta & Broccoli:Boil the penne in salted water according to package directions. Add the broccoli florets to the same pot during the last 2–3 minutes of cooking. Drain and set aside. [1, 2, 3, 4, 5]
• Sear Chicken: Heat oil or butter in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Season chicken with salt and pepper, then sauté until browned and cooked through (about 5–8 minutes). Remove chicken from the pan. [1, 2, 3]
• Prepare Sauce: In the same skillet, bring chicken broth and heavy cream to a gentle simmer. Stir in the Parmesan cheese until the sauce is smooth and slightly thickened. [1, 3, 4]
• Combine: Add the cooked pasta, broccoli, and chicken back into the skillet. Toss gently to coat everything thoroughly in the creamy sauce. [1, 2]
• Serve: Season with additional salt and pepper to taste. Garnish with chopped parsley or extra Parmesan if desired
